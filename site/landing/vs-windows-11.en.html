LuxOS vs Windows 11

Windows 11 runs almost every program and game made for PCs – but not on every PC. LuxOS is a free Linux system that runs on hardware Windows 11 refuses, without an account or a licence. Which one suits your computer depends mostly on the software you need. A fair comparison; LuxOS is beta.

When Windows 11 is the better choice

  • You depend on Windows-only software: Microsoft Office desktop with complex macros, Adobe Photoshop or Premiere, accounting, CAD or specialised work tools.
  • You play games with kernel-level anti-cheat (for example Valorant or Fortnite).
  • Your PC meets the requirements and you want support from Microsoft or the PC maker.
  • The computer must just work for someone who will not ask for help – LuxOS is still beta.

When LuxOS makes sense

  • Your PC cannot run Windows 11 – an older processor or no TPM 2.0 – and you do not want to replace a working computer (what to do after Windows 10).
  • You mostly use the web, documents, photos, music, video calls and Steam games.
  • You want no account, no ads and minimal data sent, and updates that can be undone.
  • You like the Mac's way of working: LuxOS looks and feels like macOS.

You can have both

The LuxOS installer can use free space next to Windows (dual boot): choose the system each time the computer starts. And before you install anything, LuxOS runs from a USB stick so you can check your hardware and your daily tasks.

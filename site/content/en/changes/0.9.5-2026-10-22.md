## 0.9.5 "Polaris" – 2026-10-22 (beta)

### New
- The keyboard layout switches with both Shift keys by default (the previous default Alt+Shift is converted once on update, a custom choice is kept); pressing Super alone opens Launchpad (can be turned off in Keyboard Shortcuts).
- Screenshot: the name of the selected mode and its shortcut above the bar, instant tooltips on hover; a preview with Copy, Show in Files, Save As…, Delete actions; holding Ctrl while capturing copies to the clipboard.
- New Displays and Fingerprint icons.

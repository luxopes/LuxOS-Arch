## 0.9.4 "Polaris" – 2026-10-22 (beta)

### Speed
- Images and icons are decoded directly in the app process: own `gdk-pixbuf2` package without glycin and the SVG loader `luxos-pixbuf-svg` (since gdk-pixbuf 2.44, Arch runs a bwrap sandbox + glycin process for every image; an SVG icon cost ~4 ms and 5 processes, now ~2 ms and none; login without ~40 sandboxes, Launchpad without ~120 processes, PNG 6× faster). In a VM: Dock after login in 1.9 s instead of 2.9 s, CPU after login 3.6 s instead of 5.2 s.
- luxos-hub creates the hidden Control Center and Calendar windows only after the session settles (or on the first click), gpg-agent is no longer killed and restarted at login (it runs via systemd), the wallpaper after login takes a single xfconf query, top bar script translations without loading the whole table (battery every 20 s: 70 ms → 10 ms), the LuxOS Care check after boot runs at low priority, shorter splash fade-out (0.3 s).

### Fixes
- Fingerprint: the window has a title bar with a close button again; an unknown reader error shows the technical reason. A new check `scripts/check-titlebars.sh` verifies the title bar of all apps.
- Control Center always fits on the screen (longer translations are shortened, the window is repositioned after a resize).

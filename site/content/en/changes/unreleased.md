## Coming soon

So far only in the staging test channel (`channel = "staging"` in `/etc/lux/lux.conf`).

### New
- **LSL 0.10.10** (`lsl` 0.10.10-1; previously 0.10.9): window move and resize via the window manager, screen coordinates, window state (maximized, fullscreen), fast blur and saturation of images (cairo/gdk-pixbuf, 1920×1080 in 0.15 s), bulk pixel access and helpers for transparent rounded windows built into the gtk library.
- **LSL 0.10.9** (`lsl` 0.10.9-1): window move and resize via the window manager, screen coordinates, window state (maximized, fullscreen), fast blur and saturation of images (cairo/gdk-pixbuf, 1920×1080 in 0.15 s), bulk pixel access and helpers for transparent rounded windows built into the gtk library.
- **A very bright wallpaper like on macOS: a soft shade under the menu bar and around widgets – and widgets in Desktop & Dock** (`luxos-desktop` 79, `luxos-i18n` 81, `luxos-docs` 74): on an almost white wallpaper (white, beige, a white linen texture) the white text of the menu bar and the widgets was barely visible. Now – like on macOS – the wallpaper under the bar and a little below it darkens with a smooth fade (at most 38 % black at the top, gone after 128 px); it is not a darker bar but a shade under it. The white bar text stays white with at least 4.5 : 1 contrast (WCAG AA) and switches to dark only when even that is not enough. On a bright wallpaper every widget card casts a soft dark shadow, so the light card with dark text stands out from the light desktop. The strength follows the brightness of the wallpaper (computed in advance for each wallpaper – switching workspaces with the slide loads nothing and takes as long as before): ordinary wallpapers including the light Sunrise, Lagoon and all dark ones look exactly as before, and the shade goes away by itself with a normal wallpaper. It follows the wallpaper of the active workspace, in light and dark mode, with HiDPI; it needs the compositor (the default), without it the bar behaves as before. The `luxos-bar` plugin paints the shade into the wallpaper strip of the bar and, below the bar, into its own transparent desktop window (above the wallpaper, below widgets and windows, taking no clicks – right-click on the desktop works there too). Turn it off with **Darken bright wallpaper under the menu bar and widgets** in **Desktop & Dock › Wallpaper** or `luxos-appearance desktop-scrim off` (`DESKTOP_SCRIM=0`). Widget settings no longer need Search: **Desktop & Dock** has a new **Widgets** section with **Show widgets on the desktop** (it also follows changes made in the gallery) and **Edit Widgets…**, and the desktop menu (right-click on an empty desktop) has **Edit Widgets…** under **Desktop Settings…** (GTK module `luxos-desktop-menu` in xfdesktop, not inherited by apps started from it). The transparent edge of a widget window no longer takes clicks – they go to the desktop. Tests: `test_luxos_bar_scrim.py`, `test_luxos_desktop_scrim_gui.py` (the whole desktop under Xvfb with xfwm4, xfdesktop, the bar and widgets), `test_luxos_desktop_menu.py`, extended `test_luxos_widget_glass.py`.
- **LuxAI, Lux Code, LSL IDE and the Organization network are now proprietary – still free on LuxOS** (`luxai`, `lux-code`, `lsl-ide`, `luxos-domain`; `LICENSES/`, build, website): the source code of these four components moved to a private repository and new versions are released under the LuxOS proprietary license (`LICENSES/LicenseRef-LuxOS-Proprietary.md`, third-party components in `LICENSES/NOTICE-proprietary.md`). Nothing changes on LuxOS: they are still installed free of charge as signed packages from the LuxOS repository and are included in the ISO. The rest of LuxOS stays open source under GPL-3.0-or-later (with the additional permission in `LICENSES/LuxOS-GPL-exception.md`); versions published earlier stay under the license they were released with. Building from the public repository (`./build.sh`, GitHub Actions) downloads these packages from the published repository and verifies their signatures; the FAQ and the terms of use describe what is open and what is not.
- **The Organization network (LuxOS AD) is a native LSL program** (`luxos-domain` 1.0.0-4, `luxos-greeter` 1.0.0-12): `lux domain`, the system-change helper and Settings › Organization Network are compiled LSL programs instead of Python – the same commands, files, SSSD/Kerberos settings and behaviour, no Python or PyGObject in the dependencies, the window opens faster. The login screen reads the domain state with `lux-domain status --json` (without the package, with other output or outside a domain it shows no domain line, as before).
- **Mail: write, reply, forward and send mail – and messages that look like messages** (`luxos-mail` 1.0.0-9, `luxos-i18n` 67, `luxos-docs` 52): Mail now sends mail (SMTP), not only reads it. **New Message** (Ctrl+N), **Reply** (Ctrl+R), **Reply All** (Ctrl+Shift+R) and **Forward** (Ctrl+Shift+F, with the original attachments) open a message window with From (account picker), To/Cc/Bcc with address suggestions (people you wrote to and senders in your local mail), subject, text and attachments (button, Ctrl+Shift+A or drag and drop; list with size and ×, a warning above 25 MB). Send with the button or Ctrl+Enter; replies quote the original ("On … wrote:" with `>`), keep the thread and mark the original as answered (forwarded) on the server. A signature per account, `mailto:` links with subject and body. **A written message is never lost**: it is saved on the computer 1.5 s after every change (after a crash the window comes back), to Drafts on the server after 30 s (replaced by each new version, removed after sending); closing asks "Save this message as a draft?". Without a connection the message waits in the **Outbox** and is sent automatically (30 s, 1 min … at most every 15 min, at once when the network is back); permanent errors (refused recipient, sign-in, message too large) say exactly why and nothing is sent, an Outbox message stays "Not sent" with the reason and a notification. A copy goes to Sent on the server – except for Gmail and Microsoft, which save it themselves. Own SMTP client in LSL: implicit TLS 465 and STARTTLS 587 with certificate and SNI checks, AUTH PLAIN/LOGIN, XOAUTH2/OAUTHBEARER with Google, Microsoft and LuxAI tokens, SIZE checked before sending; RFC 5322 messages in UTF-8 (RFC 2047 headers, quoted-printable/base64, RFC 2231 file names, no Bcc header). **Received mail is shown properly** (links were not clickable and formatting was poor): messages are shown in a locked-down WebKitGTK (already part of LuxOS) – HTML mail with its layout, colours, tables and attached pictures (on white paper also in dark mode when it has its own colours); plain text with clickable links, quotes as indented blocks with a coloured bar, a dimmed signature and ASCII tables in monospace. Links open in your browser (mailto: in a new message) and show their real address on hover. Remote images and tracking pixels stay blocked until **Load Images** or **Always from <sender>**; scripts, event handlers, forms, iframes, external styles and dangerous links are removed and the message page forbids network access (Content-Security-Policy), JavaScript off, no cookies. Attachments: Open / Save As…, picture previews under the text; Ctrl+F finds in the message, Ctrl+P prints, Ctrl+plus/minus/0 zooms. Ctrl+R is now Reply (like Mail on macOS); get mail with F5 or Ctrl+Shift+N. Also fixed: after a background sync rebuilt the message list, the list lost the keyboard focus and the arrow keys did nothing.
- **Mail: ready for Google app verification**: before you sign in with Google (or Microsoft), the Add Account window explains what Mail uses the access for – only to download, show and organise mail on this computer; neither your mail nor your sign-in is ever sent to LuxOS or anyone else – with a **Privacy Policy** button (luxos.lux-ai.cz/privacy). **Removing a Google account now also revokes Mail's access at Google** (token revocation, RFC 7009 – Mail disappears from myaccount.google.com/permissions); offline, the account is still removed from the computer. The remove confirmation for provider sign-ins no longer talks about a "password". The website has new pages LuxOS Mail (`/mail/`), Privacy Policy (`/privacy/`, with a section on Google user data) and Terms of Use (`/terms/`) in English and Czech, linked from the footer and checked by `scripts/check-site.py`. The verification steps for the project owner, a 120×120 logo and demo video scripts are in `docs/google-verification/`.
- **Mail: "Sign in with Google / Microsoft" instead of an app password**: adding Gmail with an app password is too complicated for most people and Outlook.com does not accept passwords for IMAP at all. Mail now signs in with the provider like Thunderbird (OAuth 2.0 for native apps, RFC 8252): for a recognised provider the Add Account window shows one main **Sign in with Google** / **Sign in with Microsoft** button (neutral text, no logos) and the password field disappears (for Google it stays under **Use an app password instead**). Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 on your own domain are detected by MX. The sign-in opens your browser, Mail listens only on `127.0.0.1` with PKCE and a random `state`, the refresh token is stored only in the keyring and the access token is kept only in memory and renewed automatically. The browser shows a small "You are signed in – back to Mail" page in the LuxOS look and the Mail window comes back to the front. When the provider stops accepting the sign-in (password change, revoked access), a calm yellow bar offers **Sign in again…** and Mail does not retry on its own. Providers are data in `/usr/share/luxos-mail/oauth-providers.json` (administrators can override them in `/etc/luxos/mail-oauth.json`; Yahoo/AOL are prepared but off). **The client IDs are still empty** – until the apps are registered with Google and Microsoft the app-password help stays; the IDs can later arrive without a new package from `https://luxos.lux-ai.cz/mail-oauth.json` (only public client IDs, never endpoints; `{"remote": false}` turns this off). Sending (SMTP) is not in Mail yet, but the sending permissions are requested already. Covered by tests with fake Google, Microsoft and IMAP servers, including a check that no tokens end up in files or logs.
- **Mail: sign in with your LuxAI account – LuxAI mailboxes without a password**: when a LuxAI account is signed in on LuxOS, the first Mail screen offers **Continue as <name>**. Mail asks the LuxAI server which mailboxes the account may use (e.g. `info@lux-ai.cz`) and adds one with a single click – no password and nothing in the keyring: before every connection the background service gets a short-lived token (15 minutes, bound to the account, this computer and the mailbox) and signs in with SASL OAUTHBEARER or XOAUTH2; the account session token goes only to the LuxAI server, never into arguments, Mail files or logs. If the account has no LuxAI mailbox, the normal add-account screen opens with the e-mail prefilled, as does **Use another account…**. Signing out of LuxAI, changing the password or removing access on the server stops new sign-ins at once; an expired sign-in shows **Sign in…**. New menu item **Add LuxAI Mailbox…**. The server side (`server/mail-oauth`: token API, Dovecot 2.4 with OAuth2 and public IMAPS, Postfix submission, fail2ban filters, deploy and rollback scripts) is tested locally and not deployed yet.
- **Mail – LuxOS's own lightweight mail client with instant new-mail notifications** (new `luxos-mail` package in `[luxos-apps]`; phase 1: reading and notifications): a small background program `luxos-mail-service` (no GTK, about 17 MB, starts with the session) keeps one connection per account and thanks to **IMAP IDLE** the server announces new mail itself – a **"New mail from …"** notification with subject and preview arrives within a second, **Open** opens Mail right at the message, several messages give one summary, already read or announced messages stay quiet and Do Not Disturb is respected. Without IDLE it checks every 5 minutes and reconnects right after the network returns or the computer wakes up. The unread count shows on the Dock icon. **The window** has three columns like Mail on macOS (mailboxes, message list with unread dots and attachment clips, message), light and dark, opens in 0.2 s even with a thousand messages, works offline from a local cache and is fully keyboard-controlled (arrows, Enter, Delete to Trash, Ctrl+Shift+U read/unread, Ctrl+F filter, Ctrl+R fetch). **Adding an account** needs only an e-mail address and password: server settings are found automatically like in Thunderbird (provider autoconfig, ISPDB, MX, DNS SRV, verified guessing), with manual setup as a fallback. **Security:** TLS always with certificate verification (no "continue anyway"), the password only in the keyring, never in a file; a wrong password is not retried; HTML messages shown as text without scripts, images or remote content (no tracking pixels). IMAP4rev1/rev2 with CONDSTORE and QRESYNC, MIME with Czech, German and Ukrainian character sets, attachments can be saved. Writing, replying and sending come in phase 2; for now Ctrl+N and `mailto:` links offer to copy the address for webmail. Mail is part of the base installation, Thunderbird stays in the Everyday set (existing systems: `lux install luxos-mail`). Written in LSL, with tests against a test IMAP server and a virtual screen; documentation in `mail.md`.

Without an open project it works only with the open files. It never reads keys, passwords, tokens, the LuxAI account or browser profiles, not even inside a project or through a symlink. The approval dialog shows the full command line and all arguments. A program that doesn't read its input no longer blocks the IDE. Long conversations no longer fail with a server error (HTTP 400) because history trimming keeps the task. Stop, Cancel and switching conversations while the agent works no longer break the conversation.

- **Security: hardening of security services and session components from an external review** (`luxos-guard` 1.0.0-12, `luxos-greeter` 1.0.0-14, `luxos-companion` 1.0.0-8, `luxos-account` 1.0.0-10, `luxos-firewall` 1.0.0-3, `luxos-care` 1.0.0-23, `luxos-terminal` 1.3.0-7, `luxos-safe` 1.0.0-10, `luxos-desktop` 1.1.0-81, `luxos-mail` 1.0.0-13): **Lux Guard** – an invalid or unusual message from a user program no longer stops the service (repeated crashes could switch Guard off), the service never waits for a client that does not read its replies, and connections, passed files and unsent replies are limited; the check for passwords fed to `sudo` runs in linear time (one long line could delay analysis by tens of seconds); USB drives with a tab, newline or backslash in their name are watched correctly; restoring from a snapshot with overwrite moves the current versions to `~/.local/share/luxos-guard/before-restore/<time>` (changes since the snapshot are not lost). The **login screen** always writes the transition frame to a new file (never through a symbolic link). **LuxOS Companion** drops unsent messages of a crashed LuxAI process. **LuxAI account** – the account cookie is not sent to a redirect target, `LUXAI_ORIGIN` over http only to this computer, an invalid server reply is a message instead of a crash, a foreign callback link no longer cancels a sign-in in progress and a new browser sign-in ends the previous one at once. The **firewall** keeps a damaged `firewall.json` as `firewall.json.damaged` before overwriting it. **LuxOS Care** does not remove the package database lock while `lux`, pamac or PackageKit runs. The **terminal** uses a new logo right after an update. The **Safe** unlocks even with hand-edited settings. **Screenshot, Emoji, Clock and Mail** connect only to a service of the same user.
- **Installing alongside Windows never damages Windows or other disks** (`luxos-installer` 1.1.0-43, `luxos-i18n` 82): manual partitioning refuses to shrink an NTFS partition (clearly, before the summary) when Windows is not fully shut down (hibernation or Fast Startup) or has a disk check scheduled – Windows would damage NTFS when resuming. `ntfsresize` never runs with `--force` any more. The shrink is tried as a dry run before the summary and done first, before any other disk is erased. Deleting or resizing partitions on an MBR disk is refused (sgdisk would silently convert it to GPT and Windows/BIOS on that disk would stop booting). The installer now needs `ntfsprogs` (Arch split `ntfsresize` out of `ntfs-3g`; it was missing on the ISO).
- **Installer: the summary lists formatting of an unlocked encrypted volume** (`luxos-installer` 1.1.0-43): formatting the contents of an existing unlocked LUKS volume (or a new LVM group in it) was missing from "Will be erased or changed"; for existing volumes "Format" is pre-selected only for `/`, otherwise "keep data".
- **Installer: the whole disk from a config file is checked** (`luxos-installer` 1.1.0-43): `DISK` for erasing a disk or installing alongside other systems must be a whole disk, not the installation medium, and (when erasing) nothing on it may be mounted – checked before the summary and again right before erasing.
- **Installer: account password and name are checked before the disk is changed** (`luxos-installer` 1.1.0-43): a password with a line break is refused, a colon or line break in the full name becomes a space (the install used to fail only after the disk was overwritten).
- **Preparing for another person (OEM) with encryption into an existing partition** (`luxos-installer` 1.1.0-43): the temporary disk key was not written to the system, so the disk was encrypted with a password nobody knew. Now it works like whole-disk encryption – the Setup Assistant replaces it with the owner's password.
- **Installer: manual partitioning on BIOS with an existing BIOS boot partition** (`luxos-installer` 1.1.0-43): a new partition on a disk that already has a BIOS boot partition is no longer refused; an erased disk `sda` is no longer confused with `sdaa`, nor `nvme0n1` with `nvme0n10`.
- **Boot: a long disk check is not a "failed start"** (`luxos-tweaks` 18): once the system disk appears, the start watch ends – systemd watches the disk check and mounting. The boot failure log on the EFI partition is at most 1 MiB and an incomplete one is deleted (a small ESP shared with Windows does not fill up).
- **Printers are not marked as removed while CUPS is unavailable** (`luxos-printing` 1.0.0-18): when cupsd was not running, sync treated all printers as removed by the user and never added them again.
- **Logging in stops only the login screen's compositor** (`luxos-desktop` 1.1.0-81): it used to stop `xcompmgr` of all users.
- **ISO: removed the broken "speakup screen reader" boot menu entry** (`espeakup` is not on the ISO).
- **Security: verified AUR drivers, community packages and updates through `lux`** (`lux` 0.3.0-28, `lsh` 1.0.0-4, `luxos-docs` 77; `[luxos-drivers]` build, `drivers/luxdrv.py`, patches for 7 Brother drivers; findings of an external code review): the check of driver install scripts (`.install`) now also covers code outside functions and permissions set at install time (setuid, including unusual spellings); forbidden commands are recognised even with another path or in quotes; source file names and allowed library paths are checked more strictly. Drivers are built as a user that cannot gain root (sudo disabled) and cannot read the signing key; driver scripts are not run in the build container. 7 Brother drivers (DCP-J1050DW, MFC-J1205W, J3540DW, J3940DW, J4335DW, J4440DW, J5340DW) got a small LuxOS patch and are verified again. Community package check (LuxAI Lite): a script too large to check completely is never installed into the system. `lux update` always confirms new versions of community packages in the system (also with `-y`) and offers their changes since the last version. `.lux` packages: updates in the staging channel use the staging index, an older index than the cached one is refused, installation is atomic (the old version comes back if the swap fails), dependency cycles end and commands outside the package are refused. `lux system`: kernel parameters no longer change the backup boot entries (previous kernel, fallback initramfs), a file with password hashes is readable only by root, a generated file that fails its own check is not kept, every error during `apply` says what already changed and how to go back, and `remove_unlisted` never removes login, desktop, network or boot loader. `lux doctor` no longer says "OK" together with the first problem it finds. `lux check` from the Store no longer blocks cleaning up a stale database lock. In lsh, `pip` as root no longer overwrites system Python packages.
- **Publishing and building** (repository scripts, not packages): publishing verifies the LuxOS signature of every package and database in all repositories (one `.sig` file used to be enough); publishing the website never deletes manually uploaded files in `download/`; composing the stable release refuses `out/` itself or a path outside it; the Secure Boot check refuses a kernel without EFI files and the ISO uses shim only with a systemd-boot really signed with the LuxOS key; signature checks no longer fail at random; `.lux` packages without icons no longer stop the build; `scripts/test-vm.sh --headless` boots the ISO (it used to boot the disk without options), `scripts/vmctl.py keys` refuses characters it cannot type before typing, and the GUI test guard stops its own D-Bus bus.
- **Backups: adding a network disk (SMB) again no longer erases the old backups** (`luxos-backup` 1.0.0-5, `luxos-i18n` 82): after "Stop Using" (the window promises that the backups on the disk are kept) and adding the same shared folder again, the backup image of this computer was always formatted again and all backups were gone – without any question. An existing image is now used as it is (an encrypted one after entering its password); it can only be erased after an explicit "Erase and Use". The same shared folder added twice is refused before anything is touched, and the server password does not stay in the keyring after a failed add.
- **Backups: the hourly backup no longer unmounts a disk the Backups window is using** (`luxos-backup` 1.0.0-5): the window (browsing, restoring) and the timer backup are separate processes, and whoever mounted the disk unmounted it at the end – even while the other one still used it. LuxOS Backups processes now record who uses a disk; only the last one unmounts it (locks it, releases the image). When mounting fails, an already unlocked disk is locked again.
- **Security: Backups – sftp server address and disk preparation** (`luxos-backup` 1.0.0-5): the server and user name of an `sftp://` address are checked and ssh gets the destination only after `--`, so an address cannot inject an ssh option. Preparing (erasing) a disk refuses the disk and partition the running system is on even under LUKS or LVM (previously only the mounted device itself was checked, not the disk below it – e.g. LuxOS installed on a USB SSD).
- **Security: the sandbox network (`--net`) has no way into the local network** (`luxos-sandbox` 1.0.0-10): only the default route stays in the namespace and the networks directly attached to the computer are blocked too, including the public IPv6 prefix of the home network from router advertisements (it was in no blocked range before); without an IPv4 default route the local IPv4 network also stayed reachable. If any step of the network setup fails, the sandbox does not start. The isolation test also checks an IPv6 address in the local network.
- **LuxOS Care: a start without a login manager is marked as good** (`luxos-care` 1.0.0-23): the post-boot check waited for `display-manager.service` even where there is none (a server, a disabled or differently started login manager) – a missing unit reports the state "inactive", which the check took as "should run". The start was then not marked as good and after a few starts the boot loader switched to the fallback kernel. It now checks whether the unit exists and is enabled.
- **LuxOS Care: a snapshot with an unreadable description keeps its kernel** (`luxos-care` 1.0.0-23): cleaning up kernel sets on the EFI partition treated a snapshot with a damaged or missing description (`.json`) as a snapshot without a kernel and deleted the kernel and initramfs the snapshot uses. When a description cannot be read the sets are now kept; they are removed only when the partition runs out of space, and before the sets of snapshots with a valid description.
- **LuxOS Recovery: reinstall lists custom system services** (`luxos-care` 1.0.0-23): custom systemd units, their drop-ins (`*.service.d/*.conf`) and masked services in `/etc/systemd/system` are not carried over to the new system and were missing from the list of settings the reinstall resets. They are listed now (and stay in the snapshot taken before the reinstall).
- **LuxOS Store: an install no longer hangs on unexpected output and cut-off images are not cached** (`luxos-store` 1.0.0-21): invalid UTF-8 in the install output (e.g. from a package script) stopped the thread reading it – the install then waited on a full pipe while holding the package database lock and the Store showed the progress forever. The output is now always read to the end. Screenshots and icons cut off during download (time limit, dropped connection) were cached forever; only complete downloads are cached now.
- **Mail: a message with broken HTML no longer freezes the app; large and unusual messages open quickly and correctly** (`luxos-mail` 1.0.0-14): a message with an invalid HTML attribute (`>` inside a tag) made the HTML cleaner loop forever – the window froze, and froze again after a restart because the message reopened by itself. A message with thousands of file-name parts (RFC 2231) was sorted in quadratic time and blocked the background service for tens of seconds (now linear: 20,000 parts in under a second). A nested part whose boundary starts with the parent's boundary was split wrongly; HTML with an unclosed `<head>` lost all text in the preview. Addresses per RFC 5322: a comma inside an encoded display name no longer creates a fake extra sender, a quoted name containing “<…>” no longer replaces the address, a group with a quoted name no longer corrupts the first address, and an IPv6 address literal (`user@[IPv6:2001:db8::1]`) is no longer cut (groups, quoted strings, comments, domain literals; encoded words are decoded only after splitting). Protection against a faulty server: limited IMAP line length and reply size, DNS SRV records only with a valid server name.
- **Mail: no double sending and no lost message in the Outbox** (`luxos-mail` 1.0.0-14): when the connection dropped right after the message was handed to the server (no reply), Mail sent it again by itself – now it marks it "may have been sent, check Sent" and you decide. When the service stopped before taking over the message, it stayed both in the compose window and in the Outbox and went out by itself after 10 minutes (twice after pressing Send again) – now it stays only in the window. The compose window no longer stays in "Sending…" forever when the service does not start. In the Outbox the selection shifted when another message was queued, so Delete / Send Now hit a different message; a message opened from the Outbox for editing was lost when the window was closed without changes; a draft that failed to save on the server was dropped on close. Without UIDPLUS the server also deleted messages another program had marked as deleted; flagging or deleting from an outdated list (mailbox re-created meanwhile) could hit a different message – now it is refused and the list refreshes. Two attachments with the same name no longer overwrite each other when forwarding, removing an account checks its id, and Mail no longer quits after closing a compose window while the main window is open again.
- **LuxDrop: more reliable trusted-device list and LocalSend receiving** (`luxdrop` 1.0.0-9): simultaneous writes (receiver, window, service) could overwrite changes or corrupt the trusted-device list or the history – writes are now locked. One device can no longer take all LocalSend receive connections, the PIN has a shared limit across all addresses in addition to the per-address one, and a broken reply from the receiver no longer crashes sending (the transfer ends as failed).
- **Server: rolling back the mail sign-in deployment also restores the Dovecot setting, and the install statistics keep their history when one entry is invalid** (`server/mail-oauth`, `server/countme`): `remote-rollback.sh` did not restore the `auth-passwdfile.conf.ext` change; countme moves the original file aside unchanged and keeps the valid entries.

The icon is slightly larger (at 1280×800 the bolt was only 3×6 px). The same on the luxos-greeter login screen. Also, a battery without a readable capacity is no longer shown as "-1 %" or 0 %. Tests: icon rendering at every charge level, wallpaper and scale (`xfce4-screensaver/tests/test-lock-battery.c`, `luxos-greeter/tests/gui-battery.py`) and the real lock screen under Xvfb (`xfce4-screensaver/tests/gui-battery.py`).
- **Help: Displays on its own page** (`luxos-docs` 75): the Settings page outgrew the size limit, the Displays app now has its own page.
- **Widgets: the Now Playing card no longer jumps around or disappears, and its button does what it shows** (`luxos-desktop` 1.1.0-80; complaint "when I play music from the widget it stops after a while anyway, the widget jumps around and disappears" – Firefox and switching workspaces): on every new page or new video (page reload, next YouTube video) Firefox drops its MPRIS player and registers it again right away – the Now Playing card vanished for a moment and came back, and because a hidden card did not keep its slot in the default layout, all cards below it jumped up and back.

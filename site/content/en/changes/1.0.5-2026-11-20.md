## 1.0.5 "Sirius" – 2026-11-20

Release 1.0.5 "Sirius": LuxOS works fully on touch screens – tablets, 2-in-1 laptops and touch monitors, also without a physical keyboard. Installed systems get it through `lux update`.

### New
- **Touch screens and tablets, also without a keyboard** (`luxos-desktop` 1.1.0-97, `luxos-greeter` 1.0.0-16, `luxos-i18n` 98, `luxos-branding` 1.1.0-34, `luxos-docs` 95; new dependencies `onboard`, `touchegg`, `iio-sensor-proxy`): on a device with a touch screen LuxOS turns on everything needed at sign-in (`luxos-touch`); computers without touch are not changed. The **on-screen keyboard** (Onboard) appears by itself when you tap a text field and hides again, is docked at the bottom and windows shrink above it; with a physical keyboard connected it does not pop up by itself, and the floating button, the **Keyboard** tile in Control Center or a four-finger swipe up show it any time. The **sign-in screen** has a Keyboard button (without a physical keyboard the keyboard is shown right away), the **lock screen** has its keyboard button turned on, and the installer's live system works the same – LuxOS can be installed by touch only. **Gestures** (Touchégg): three fingers up for Launchpad, down for the desktop, sideways to switch workspaces, a three-finger pinch closes the window, a two-finger tap is a right click. **Screen rotation** follows the sensor, touch rotates with it and stays on the built-in screen also with a monitor connected; **Rotation Lock** tile. New manual page "Touch screens and tablets", What's New 1.0.5 in 13 languages.
- **Hot corners** (`luxos-desktop` 1.1.0-99, default corners 1.1.0-101, `luxos-i18n` 99, `luxos-docs` 98; a wish from Reddit): moving the pointer into a corner of the screen and keeping it there for a moment (about 0.3 s) runs an action – by default the top left corner opens **Launchpad** and the other corners do nothing (**Show Desktop**, for example in the bottom right corner like in Windows, can be turned on – it was the default only briefly in staging and triggered by accident on the way to the Dock). Each corner can be set in **Desktop & Dock › Hot Corners** (Launchpad, Show Desktop, Control Center, Lock Screen, Start Screen Saver, nothing); a change applies within two seconds without signing out. A corner runs again only after the pointer leaves it, passing through the top left corner on the way to the LuxOS menu does not open Launchpad, and on a touch device without a keyboard the corners are off. New manual page "Hot corners".
- **Touch, after the first tester on a real device** (`luxos-desktop` 1.1.0-100, `luxos-i18n` 100, `luxos-docs` 97; new dependency `unclutter`): **Firefox** gets real touch events on a touch device – dragging a finger scrolls pages and YouTube (before, touch acted as a mouse and selected text); the **pointer hides when you touch** the screen and comes back with the mouse; the **on-screen keyboard** pops up by itself also on convertibles that keep their built-in keyboard connected (it hides as soon as you type on the physical one) and is **bigger** (about 40 % of the screen height in landscape, 30 % in portrait; your own height is kept); the accessibility bus is turned on right away, so the keyboard also appears in apps that were already running; a **safeguard against "dead" touch** – when Onboard leaves the touch screen detached from the pointer after simulating a click, LuxOS reattaches it within seconds (`luxos-touch guard`); switching workspaces also with **four fingers** (with an app open, three fingers often scroll in the app).
- **Display scale 100–200 %** (Displays › Scale, like in Windows; also asked for by the same tester): 125 and 150 % enlarge text, the menu bar, Dock icons, the cursor and window title bars including their buttons (WhiteSur `-hdpi` frames, easier to hit with a finger), 175 and 200 % switch GTK to 2×; Text Size is relative to the scale, your own menu bar height and Dock size are kept.

## 0.9.3 "Polaris" – 2026-10-22 (beta)

### New
- Full battery saver: no CPU turbo, 60 Hz display, lower brightness, no animations, audio and USB power saving, paused background tasks (the system part runs as a service with a polkit rule). Data saver also pauses reflector and key synchronisation on a metered connection.
- Battery and Power: "Don't sleep while playing music or video" (closing the lid only turns off the screen); charge cycle count – and it says so when the battery does not report it.
- Terminal: `about os`, `about system`, `adduser`, `deluser`, `addgroup`, `delgroup`, `service`, `update-grub`, `lsb_release`; hints for `apt`, `dnf`, `ifconfig`, `netstat` and more.
- Bluetooth: renaming the computer. Installer: optional secure erase of the target space (in hardware or with zeros).
- macOS-style Alt+Tab.

### Fixes
- Playback controls in Control Center respond instantly (MPRIS events instead of polling).
- Lock screen after wake-up: no black bar at the bottom and no shifted dialog (an xfce4-screensaver bug, fixed with a GTK module).
- Window names "What's New" and "Screenshot" in the top bar.

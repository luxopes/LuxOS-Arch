## 1.0.4 "Sirius" – 2026-11-21

Bug-fix release 1.0.4 "Sirius".

### Fixed
- **Logging in without a black flash** (`luxos-desktop` 1.1.0-95, `luxos-docs` 1.0.0-92): while the desktop was loading after login, the screen briefly went black. The cause was Battery Saver: after every login (and after connecting a monitor) `luxos-displays auto` switched a built-in display with a higher refresh rate (e.g. 144 Hz) to 60 Hz even when the "Lower the screen to 60 Hz" option (Battery & Power) was off – and changing the refresh rate always blanks the screen for a moment. The rate now changes only with that option, and the original rate is saved so that turning Battery Saver off restores it. A second gap: when the window manager started more than 3 s after login (slow session start, cold disk), the transition ended too early and the compositor drew black until the wallpaper was painted – the transition now waits up to 6 s for the window manager (it always ends within 10 s, and input goes through it). With a compositor that starts late, the transition dissolves only after the compositor has run for 0.4 s (in its first frames it still draws a black background). The screen-capture test of the transition has a new variant with a late window manager.

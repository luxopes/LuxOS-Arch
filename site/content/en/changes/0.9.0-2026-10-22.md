## 0.9.0 "Polaris" – 2026-10-22 (beta)

The first numbered version.

### Look and desktop
- A macOS-style desktop on top of Xfce: top bar with menus and window icons, Dock, Launchpad, Search, Control Center, notifications, calendar.
- Own LuxOS icons, wallpapers and logo, light/dark/automatic mode, boot splash and login screen.
- LuxOS Setup Assistant at the first login.

### Apps
- LuxOS Store, LuxAI (with a LuxAI account), Lux Code, LSL IDE, Calculator, Camera, Scanner, Printers and Scanners, Drivers, System Monitor, Bluetooth, Battery and Power, Vault (encrypted folder), LuxDrop (LocalSend-compatible), Feedback.

### System
- `lux` – own package manager on top of libalpm (instead of the pacman command), signed `[luxos]` and `[luxos-apps]` repositories.
- Automatic hardware and driver detection (NVIDIA by generation including older cards, sound, cameras, Wi‑Fi), printing and scanning without setup.
- Out-of-memory protection (earlyoom), data and battery saver, automatic maintenance.
- 13 languages.

### Installer
- Whole disk, free space next to another system, an existing partition, or manual partitioning (LVM, LUKS2, separate partitions).
- App sets (Everyday, Programming, Video and Photo, Games, Security and Networking).
- LuxAI account right in the installer: sign-in with a password, a code from e-mail, or creating a new account.
